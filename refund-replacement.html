Refund & Replacement Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026
Important: This policy is designed for a personalised, made-to-order physical product. It does not attempt to remove statutory consumer rights. If a card is defective, incorrectly printed by us, or damaged in transit, contact us promptly so we can investigate and provide the remedy required by this policy and applicable law.

1. Cancellation before production

You may request cancellation as soon as possible after payment. If production has not started and the order can reasonably be cancelled, we will process a refund of the amount paid for that cancelled order, subject to any payment-processing amount that cannot be recovered by us where legally permissible.

Once a personalised card has entered printing, lamination or other production, cancellation for change of mind is generally not available because the product has been made specifically from your submitted information.

2. Defective, unreadable or incorrectly printed card

If we print the wrong information despite receiving the correct information from you, or if the card arrives materially defective or unreadable due to a production defect, we will replace the affected card(s) at no charge, subject to verification.

Where the issue can reasonably be resolved by correcting the digital profile rather than reprinting the physical card, we may do that instead.

3. Damage in transit

If the card arrives damaged, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible with your order reference and clear photographs of the packaging and product. We may request additional evidence to determine whether the damage occurred during fulfilment or delivery.

4. Customer-entered errors

If the card was produced correctly according to the information you supplied, but you later discover that you supplied the wrong phone number, vehicle number, name, photograph, address or other information, the free defect replacement does not apply. A corrected replacement may be available for a replacement fee plus applicable delivery cost.

This includes requests caused by a change of vehicle, change of phone number, change of personal details or a preference change after production.

5. Lost, stolen or customer-damaged cards

Loss, theft, deliberate damage, improper mounting, ordinary wear beyond the product's reasonable expected use, or damage caused by chemicals, heat, scraping or other customer actions is not treated as a manufacturing defect.

If your card is lost or stolen, contact us promptly. Where the digital system permits, we may deactivate or reassign the associated QR profile. A physical replacement may be offered for a fee.

6. QR code or digital profile not working

If the QR code supplied by us does not scan under ordinary conditions, or the associated MoveMe page is materially unavailable because of an issue within our reasonable control, contact support. We will first attempt to diagnose and correct the digital issue. If a physical reprint is necessary because of our production error, we will provide the applicable replacement.

Failures caused by the customer's phone, camera, browser, internet connection, third-party network, external service outage or deliberate alteration of the card are not automatically treated as product defects.

7. Delivery problems

If an order does not arrive within a reasonable period, contact us with the order reference. We will investigate the shipment. If the parcel is confirmed lost after dispatch and the loss is not caused by an incorrect customer address or repeated delivery failure attributable to the customer, we will arrange the remedy applicable to the order, which may include replacement or refund.

8. Refund method and timing

Where a refund is approved, we will normally use the original payment method or another lawful method agreed with the customer. Processing time can depend on the payment provider or bank.

9. How to request a refund or replacement

Email geetsehgal1990@proton.me or call +91 78383 38058. Include your order reference, the problem, and photographs or other evidence where relevant. We may ask for the affected card to be returned before issuing a replacement where reasonably necessary.

10. Abuse and repeated claims

We may investigate repeated, contradictory or apparently fraudulent claims before approving a refund or replacement. This does not affect genuine consumer remedies available under applicable law.

11. Statutory rights

This policy is subject to the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and other applicable law. Nothing here limits a remedy that the law requires us to provide.
