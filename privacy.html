Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026
What we collect: information needed to process your order and create your vehicle contact card — including contact, delivery, vehicle and optional profile information, plus payment verification information.

1. Who controls your data

For this website and its order process, MoveMe / Scan & Call acts as the party responsible for the personal data processing described in this policy. Privacy questions can be sent to geetsehgal1990@proton.me.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact data: name, phone number and email address.
  • Delivery data: delivery address, city and pincode.
  • Vehicle/profile data: car number, bike number, optional bio lines and optional photograph.
  • Payment verification data: UPI transaction reference (UTR) and the payment screenshot you upload.
  • Order data: products, quantities, price, order reference, fulfilment status and support correspondence.
  • Technical data: limited information that may be generated by web hosting, security, browser or network services when you access the website.

3. Why we use it

We use personal data to verify payment, process and deliver orders, create and maintain the QR-linked contact page, provide customer support, handle refunds/replacements, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not need your optional photograph to process an order. If you choose to provide one, it may be displayed on your QR-linked contact page according to the product design.

4. The QR page is intentionally shareable

The central feature of this product is that a person who scans the QR code can access the contact information you chose to publish through the vehicle card. Do not put sensitive, secret or unnecessary personal information in your profile text or photograph.

In particular, do not use the QR profile to publish passwords, government identification numbers, financial information, medical information or other sensitive information that is not necessary for someone trying to contact you about your vehicle.

5. Payment providers and service providers

Payments are initiated through UPI and payment information may be processed by payment, banking, hosting, storage, email, analytics or other service providers used to operate the business. We only provide those providers with information reasonably needed for their function and require appropriate handling to the extent applicable.

6. Data retention

We retain order and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfilment, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Personal data associated with an inactive service may be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer reasonably necessary, subject to legal or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal data associated with your order, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where applicable, or ask us to deactivate/update your QR-linked profile. Some data may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where processing is based on consent, applicable law may give you a right to withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not necessarily undo processing that was already lawful or prevent processing that is otherwise permitted or required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the data and the size of the service. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

The service is intended for purchasers who can lawfully enter into the purchase transaction. We do not knowingly design the service to collect children's personal data for independent use. If you believe a child's information has been submitted improperly, contact us so we can review it.

10. Third-party links and services

The website may use or link to third-party services. Their own privacy terms may apply to information processed directly by them. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third parties outside our control.

11. Legal framework

We intend this policy to operate consistently with applicable Indian privacy and consumer-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules as and when their relevant provisions apply. The implementation of India's DPDP framework is phased, so specific statutory obligations may become applicable at different times.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices or applicable law changes. The current version will be published on this website with an updated effective date.
